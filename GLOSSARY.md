# justfredrik.com

Fredrik Frodlund's personal blog. It is the successor to frippz.se and carries all of its content forward under a new name.

## Language

**Post**:
A dated piece of writing with a title, a short description and a body, published on the blog.
_Avoid_: Article, entry, blog post

**Tag**:
A topic label attached to a Post; a Post can have several, and each Tag has its own listing of Posts.
_Avoid_: Category, topic, label

**Draft**:
A Post that has been saved but not published; it is not visible on the blog.
_Avoid_: Unpublished post, WIP

**Live Preview**:
A Post or Draft shown as it would appear on the blog, beside it while it is being edited, including changes not yet saved.
_Avoid_: Preview mode, draft preview

**Lab**:
A self-contained interactive front-end demo with a short explanatory note, shown outside the blog's usual page frame.
_Avoid_: Experiment, demo, playground
